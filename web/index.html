Try it on a real recording

These spirals were drawn with a pen on a Wacom tablet by people with Parkinson's and by controls, from a public research dataset. There are no essential tremor recordings in it. The same Python code that analyses your phone drawing analyses these.

The tablet reports pixels, not millimetres. We assume 0.204 mm per pixel (Wacom Cintiq 12WX), so every mm value here carries that assumption. Hz values don't depend on it.

Where it works, where it doesn't

Evidence

Method

  1. Points from the screen (Pointer Events, with coalesced events where the browser offers them) are converted to millimetres with your card calibration.
  2. The trace is resampled to a uniform 100 Hz grid.
  3. A 2-D band-pass keeps 3.5–12 Hz movement (raised-cosine edges, 2.5→3.5 Hz and 12→14 Hz), which removes the slow drawing motion and leaves the tremor.
  4. A Welch spectrum (4 s Hann segments, 0.25 Hz bins) of x and y power combined. The peak in 4–12 Hz is reported only if it stands at least 4× above the median of the band; otherwise "no dominant rhythm".
  5. Amplitude is peak-to-peak along the local principal axis of the filtered movement in 0.5 s windows (median over windows).

The change verdict in History compares the last 7 days with the 7 before, on log amplitude, and calls a change only if it exceeds both your own day-to-day variation and a published minimal-detectable-change floor (Elble & Ellenbogen 2017). It needs at least 3 drawings in each week.

Limitations

  • Not tested on anyone with essential tremor yet. The public data has Parkinson's and controls only, drawn with a pen on a tablet, not a finger on a phone.
  • Finger tracing on glass is not pen on paper. Phones report touch at different rates; some browsers send too few points, and the app then refuses to measure.
  • Spiral drawing captures action tremor. It does not measure rest tremor, the Parkinson's hallmark.
  • It never diagnoses. It measures and trends; your neurologist decides.
  • Millimetres depend on the card calibration. Uncalibrated results are labelled.
